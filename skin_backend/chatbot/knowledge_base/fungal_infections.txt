Condition: Fungal Infections (Tinea, Ringworm, Candidiasis)

General guidance:
- Keep the affected area clean and dry, since fungal infections thrive in warm, moist environments.
- Avoid sharing towels, clothing, or personal items to reduce spread to others.
- Wear breathable fabrics and change out of sweaty clothing promptly.
- Avoid scratching the area, as this can spread the infection to other parts of the body.
- A dermatologist can confirm the type of fungal infection and the right treatment approach.
