Condition: Seborrheic Keratoses and other Benign Tumors

General guidance:
- These growths are often harmless, but any new or changing growth is still worth having checked.
- Avoid picking, scratching, or attempting to remove the growth at home.
- Track any changes in size, color, or texture over time.
- Protect the area from unnecessary friction (e.g., from clothing or jewelry).
- A dermatologist can confirm whether the growth is benign and whether monitoring or removal is appropriate.
