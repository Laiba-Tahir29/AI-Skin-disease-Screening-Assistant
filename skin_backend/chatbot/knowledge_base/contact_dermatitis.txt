Condition: Contact Dermatitis (including Poison Ivy)

General guidance:
- Try to identify and avoid the substance that triggered the reaction (plants, soaps, metals, fragrances, etc.).
- Gently rinse the affected area with cool water and avoid scrubbing.
- Avoid tight clothing or fabrics that may rub against the irritated skin.
- Keep the area clean and avoid scratching, as this can lead to further irritation or infection.
- If the reaction is spreading, swelling significantly, or affecting the face/eyes, seek medical attention promptly.
