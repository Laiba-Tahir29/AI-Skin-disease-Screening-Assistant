Condition: Vascular Tumors

General guidance:
- Avoid trauma or friction to the area, as vascular growths can sometimes bleed if irritated.
- Track any changes in size, color, or texture over time and note when the growth first appeared.
- Avoid picking at or attempting to treat the area with home remedies.
- Protect the area from sun exposure if it is in a commonly exposed location.
- A dermatologist should evaluate vascular growths to determine their nature and whether monitoring or further care is needed.
