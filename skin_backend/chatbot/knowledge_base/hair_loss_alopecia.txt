Condition: Hair Loss / Alopecia

General guidance:
- Avoid tight hairstyles, excessive heat styling, or harsh chemical treatments, which can add stress to hair and scalp.
- Gentle handling of hair when washing or brushing can help reduce further loss.
- Track the pattern of hair loss (patches vs. overall thinning) and how long it has been happening.
- Stress, diet, and overall health can all play a role, so general wellbeing is worth paying attention to.
- A dermatologist can help identify the type of hair loss and appropriate next steps.
