Condition: Atopic Dermatitis

General guidance:
- Keep skin well moisturized with a fragrance-free moisturizer, especially after bathing.
- Avoid long, hot showers and harsh soaps, which can dry out and irritate the skin further.
- Identify and avoid personal triggers such as certain fabrics, stress, or environmental allergens where possible.
- Keep fingernails short to reduce skin damage from scratching.
- A dermatologist can help build a long-term skincare routine suited to sensitive, atopic-prone skin.
