Condition: Psoriasis, Lichen Planus, and related conditions

General guidance:
- Moisturize regularly to help reduce dryness and scaling associated with these conditions.
- Avoid known personal triggers such as stress, certain medications, or skin injury, which can worsen flare-ups for some people.
- Gentle sun exposure may help some skin conditions, but always use sun protection and avoid burning.
- Avoid picking or scratching plaques, as this can worsen the area (Koebner phenomenon).
- A dermatologist can help create a long-term management plan tailored to the specific condition.
