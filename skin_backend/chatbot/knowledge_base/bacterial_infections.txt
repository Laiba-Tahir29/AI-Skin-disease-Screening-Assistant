Condition: Cellulitis, Impetigo and other Bacterial Infections

General guidance:
- Keep the affected area clean and covered with a clean, breathable bandage if possible.
- Avoid touching, scratching, or draining the area yourself.
- Wash hands frequently to avoid spreading the infection to other areas or people.
- Watch for spreading redness, warmth, swelling, or fever, as these can indicate the infection is worsening.
- Bacterial skin infections, especially cellulitis, should be evaluated by a doctor promptly, as they can spread quickly.
