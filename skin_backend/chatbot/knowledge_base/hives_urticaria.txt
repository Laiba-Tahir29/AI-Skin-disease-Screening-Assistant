Condition: Urticaria (Hives)

General guidance:
- Try to identify possible triggers such as certain foods, heat, stress, or new products, and avoid them where possible.
- Loose, breathable clothing can help reduce irritation on affected skin.
- Avoid hot showers or scratching, as both can worsen itching and swelling.
- Cool compresses may help ease discomfort in the affected area.
- If hives are associated with difficulty breathing, swelling of the face/lips, or spread rapidly, seek emergency medical care immediately — this can be a sign of a serious allergic reaction.
