Condition: Bullous Disease

General guidance:
- Avoid friction, tight clothing, or activities that could rupture blisters, as this increases risk of infection.
- Keep the area clean and avoid popping blisters yourself.
- Loose, soft clothing can help reduce irritation on affected skin.
- Track how quickly new blisters appear and where, as this pattern is useful for a specialist.
- Bullous conditions often need prompt dermatologist evaluation, especially if blisters are spreading or the area is large.
