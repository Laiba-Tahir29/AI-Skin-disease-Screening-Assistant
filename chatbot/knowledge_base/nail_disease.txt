Condition: Nail Fungus and other Nail Disease

General guidance:
- Keep nails clean, dry, and trimmed, as moisture can worsen fungal nail conditions.
- Avoid sharing nail clippers, files, or footwear to reduce spread.
- Wear breathable footwear and moisture-wicking socks if the feet are affected.
- Avoid picking at or cutting into the affected nail, as this can worsen the condition or cause infection.
- A dermatologist can confirm the cause and appropriate care for persistent nail changes.
