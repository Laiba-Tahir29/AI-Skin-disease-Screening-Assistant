Condition: Lupus and other Connective Tissue Diseases

General guidance:
- These conditions can involve more than the skin, so ongoing changes in energy, joints, or overall health should also be mentioned to a doctor.
- Sun exposure can trigger flare-ups for some connective tissue conditions, so sun protection (sunscreen, hats, shade) is generally advisable.
- Avoid harsh or irritating skincare products on affected areas.
- Keep a simple log of when symptoms appear or worsen, as this is useful information for a specialist.
- A dermatologist or rheumatologist should evaluate these conditions, as they often require coordinated care.
