Condition: Eczema

General guidance:
- Moisturize frequently, especially after bathing, to help maintain the skin barrier.
- Avoid harsh soaps, fragranced products, and very hot water, which can worsen dryness and irritation.
- Identify and avoid personal triggers such as certain fabrics, allergens, or stress where possible.
- Keep nails trimmed to minimize skin damage from scratching.
- A dermatologist can help build a long-term routine and identify triggers specific to your skin.
