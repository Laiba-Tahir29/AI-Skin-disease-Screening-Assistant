Condition: Exanthems and Drug Eruptions

General guidance:
- If a new rash appeared after starting a new medication, note the timing and mention it to a doctor as soon as possible.
- Avoid further exposure to any suspected trigger substance until evaluated.
- Keep the area clean and avoid scratching to reduce irritation.
- Track whether the rash is spreading, and note any fever or feeling unwell alongside it.
- Widespread rashes, especially with fever or blistering, should be evaluated by a doctor promptly.
