Condition: Acne and Rosacea

General guidance:
- Avoid harsh scrubbing, strong exfoliants, or alcohol-based products, as these can worsen irritation.
- Use a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser and non-comedogenic moisturizer.
- Avoid touching or picking at the affected area, as this can increase irritation and scarring risk.
- For rosacea-prone skin, triggers like spicy food, alcohol, extreme temperatures, and sun exposure may worsen redness — sunscreen is generally recommended.
- A dermatologist can help identify the right long-term routine and whether further evaluation is needed.
