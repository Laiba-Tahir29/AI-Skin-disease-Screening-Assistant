Condition: Warts, Molluscum and other Viral Skin Infections

General guidance:
- Avoid picking, scratching, or shaving over the affected area, as this can spread the infection to other areas.
- Avoid sharing towels, razors, or personal items to reduce spread to others.
- Keep the area clean and avoid unnecessary touching.
- These infections are common and often resolve over time, but persistent or spreading lesions should be evaluated.
- A dermatologist can advise on appropriate care and whether removal is recommended.
