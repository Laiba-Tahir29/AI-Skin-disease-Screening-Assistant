Condition: Herpes, HPV and other Sexually Transmitted Skin Conditions

General guidance:
- Avoid skin-to-skin or sexual contact with the affected area until it has been evaluated, to reduce risk of transmission.
- Keep the area clean and avoid touching or picking at it.
- These conditions are common and manageable — a healthcare provider can offer confidential, judgment-free evaluation and guidance.
- Partner communication and testing may be recommended once you've spoken with a healthcare provider.
- A doctor or sexual health clinic can provide proper evaluation and next steps.
