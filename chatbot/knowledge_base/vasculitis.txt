Condition: Vasculitis

General guidance:
- Vasculitis can be linked to broader health conditions, so any accompanying symptoms (fever, joint pain, fatigue) should also be mentioned to a doctor.
- Avoid activities that put extra pressure or friction on affected areas.
- Keep track of when new spots or patches appear and how they change over time.
- Avoid self-treating the skin in isolation, since evaluation of overall health may be needed.
- A doctor should evaluate vasculitis promptly, as it can sometimes indicate an underlying condition needing attention.
