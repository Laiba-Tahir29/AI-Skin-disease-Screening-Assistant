Condition: Systemic Disease (skin signs of a broader internal condition)

General guidance:
- Skin changes linked to systemic disease are often a sign that the whole body should be evaluated, not just the skin.
- Keep track of any other symptoms (fatigue, fever, joint pain, weight changes) and mention them to a doctor.
- Avoid self-treating the skin symptom in isolation, since the underlying cause may need broader medical attention.
- Maintain a gentle skincare routine and avoid irritating the affected area further.
- A general physician or dermatologist should be consulted for a full evaluation.
