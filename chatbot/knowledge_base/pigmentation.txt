Condition: Light Diseases and Disorders of Pigmentation

General guidance:
- Protect the affected area from direct sun exposure using broad-spectrum sunscreen and covering clothing, since pigmentation changes can be more sensitive to UV light.
- Avoid harsh scrubbing, bleaching creams, or unregulated skin-lightening products, as these can irritate the skin further.
- Keep a simple, gentle skincare routine and avoid frequent changes of products.
- Track any changes in size, shape, or color of the affected area and note when it started.
- A dermatologist can properly evaluate the type of pigmentation change and the right next steps.
