Condition: Scabies, Lyme Disease and other Infestations/Bites

General guidance:
- Avoid close skin contact and sharing clothing or bedding with others until evaluated, as some infestations are contagious.
- Wash clothing and bedding in hot water if scabies or similar infestation is suspected.
- For tick bites or suspected Lyme disease, note the date of the bite and watch for a spreading rash, fever, or fatigue.
- Avoid scratching, as this can lead to secondary skin infection.
- Prompt medical evaluation is recommended, especially for tick-related exposure or spreading rashes.
